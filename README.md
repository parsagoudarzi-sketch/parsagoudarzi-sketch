### 🏠 Barghino — Smart Home Automation Ecosystem
**Originated in 2025 · Actively evolving**

Barghino is my long-term project to build a flexible smart home automation platform, combining embedded devices, centralized control, and a modern software interface.

**Core architecture & development roadmap**
- **Central Hub:** A central unit coordinating connected switches and automation devices.
- **Local-First Control:** Designed to keep essential home control available without depending on cloud services.
- **Embedded Communication:** Exploring reliable communication between the central unit and distributed microcontroller-based nodes.
- **Web-Based Interface:** A unified control panel for devices, rooms, and automation workflows.
- **Network Resilience:** Configurable connectivity and recovery strategies.
- **Data & Configuration:** Persistent settings, device information, and event logging.
- **Future Intelligence:** Rule-based automation, usage analysis, and potential AI-assisted control.

The long-term goal is to evolve Barghino into a maintainable, extensible, and potentially commercial smart home platform.

`ESP32` `ESP8266` `IoT` `Web Technologies` `Embedded Networking`
